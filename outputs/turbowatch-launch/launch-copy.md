## Fact check before public use

Delivered product features include photo/video discovery, search by posts/people/usernames/tags, format filters and sorting, shorts playback, likes/comments/reposts/sharing, collections, profiles, messaging, and real Supabase account/recovery code. Sample-library screenshots and pictured accounts are fictional and do not demonstrate live community adoption. No public launch, user totals, performance statistics, testimonials, AI scoring or predictive claims are made here.

Public authentication still requires unrestricted email delivery and production access verification. Custom SMTP is currently disabled and the default sender is limited to project-team addresses. Vercel protection blocks independent live verification. These assets were prepared locally and were not posted, emailed or deployed.
